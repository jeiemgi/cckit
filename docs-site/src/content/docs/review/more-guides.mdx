The sidebar lists the guides most teams follow first. These three cover the safety side of review:
what may not be committed, and what an agent may change without asking. Each one is a short
step-by-step page with a way to check the result.

## Keep secrets and private data out of commits

<CardGrid>
  <LinkCard title="Stop agents committing secrets" href="/review/stop-committing-secrets/" description="Wire the secret guard as a pre-commit hook and prove it blocks a key. Beginner, 5 minutes." />
  <LinkCard title="Keep private data out" href="/review/keep-private-data-out/" description="List the organisation names, hosts and emails the guard must block. Beginner, 5 minutes." />
</CardGrid>

Do the first before the second: the private list is read by the hook the first guide turns on.

## Guides in the sidebar

- [Choose your gate command](/review/gate-command/): the one command that proves a change is sound.
- [Turn repeated review findings into checks](/review/gates/): checks that fail when a known defect comes back.
- [The secret guard](/review/secret-guard/): what the guard blocks and where it runs.
- [Agent review before a merge](/review/agent-review/): a second agent reads each pull request.
- [QA on a running stack](/review/qa-lane/): a person tries the change and records a verdict.

Related guides in other areas: [Run checks with hooks](/development/checks-with-hooks/) for what
runs before an agent's command, and [Merge, close and clean up](/ship/merge-and-close/) for what
happens after a change passes review.
