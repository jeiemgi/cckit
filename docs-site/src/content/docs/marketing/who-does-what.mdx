When you hand work over, the assistants do the preparing and people keep the decisions. This page
says who does each part, so a piece always has one person holding it.

Two groups take part: the people on your team, and the assistants that come with the project.
People decide. Assistants prepare. The examples on these pages come from one shop, Pilot Light
Bakery: its owner writes, and the colleague at the counter approves.

## People

| Person | Does | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Writer** (you) | Says what to make, answers your assistant's questions, sends the review link. | Claude Code and a GitHub account. |
| **Approver** | Reads the review, comments, approves or asks for changes. | A GitHub account. A browser is enough. |
| **Technical person** | Sets the project up once. Fixes access problems. | A terminal. See [Give the keys](/marketing/get-set-up/). |

One person can be writer on one piece and approver on another. The same person should not approve
their own piece. The reason is on [Review and approval](/marketing/review-and-approval/).

## Assistants

The marketing profile gives your assistant four roles. You do not call them by name; your
assistant hands work to the right one. You can also ask for one directly:

<Chat
  title="asking the researcher to check a claim about the Easter loaf"
  say="Can the researcher check this line before I use it? “Sweet braided loaves have been baked for Easter for centuries.” I want the sources."
  means="The researcher owns facts and sources. You still decide whether the line stays in the post."
>
  I passed it to the researcher. It will come back with what it found, where each fact comes from
  and anything it could not confirm.
</Chat>

| Assistant | Owns | Ask it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Planner** (named PM in the tools) | The list of work: what is open, what is stuck, what comes next. | "Where are we?", "What should I work on next?", a campaign plan. |
| **Editor** | The written word: structure, clarity, voice, headlines. | An outline, a draft, a tighter version, a check against the brand voice. |
| **Designer** | How things look: layout, color, type, images. | Feedback on a page layout, a description of the visual a post needs. |
| **Researcher** | Facts and sources. | A competitor scan, a fact check, a short brief with sources. |

Your assistant designs and builds pages. No design tool is connected, so it does not make photos,
logos or illustrations: you supply those, and the designer describes the visual a piece needs.

## Who decides

| Decision | Who |
| --- | --- |
| What to make | The writer, or whoever sets the team's priorities. |
| What a draft says | The writer. Your assistant proposes; you accept or change. |
| Whether a piece is approved | The approver. Never your assistant. |
| The brand voice | The team, through a review. See [Set your brand voice](/marketing/brand-voice/). |
| When and where it is published | Your team, outside cckit. |
