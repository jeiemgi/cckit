title: Who does what

Work stalls when nobody knows whose turn it is: the writer waits for feedback, the approver does
not know a draft is ready, and a decision gets made by whoever answered last. This page says who
does each part, so a piece always has one person holding it.

Two groups do the work: the people on your team, and the assistants Claude brings to the project.
People decide. Assistants prepare.

## People

| Person | Does | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Writer** (you) | Says what to make, shapes the draft with Claude, asks for a review. | Claude Code and a GitHub account. |
| **Approver** | Reads the review, comments, approves or asks for changes. | A GitHub account. A browser is enough. |
| **Technical person** | Sets the project up once. Fixes access problems. | A terminal. See [Get set up](/marketing/get-set-up/). |

One person can be writer on one piece and approver on another. The same person should not approve
their own piece. The reason is on [Review and approval](/marketing/review-and-approval/).

## Assistants

The marketing profile gives Claude four roles. You do not call them by name; Claude hands work to
the right one. You can also ask for one directly: "have the researcher check this number".

| Assistant | Owns | Ask it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Planner** (named PM in the tools) | The list of work: what is open, what is stuck, what comes next. | "Where are we?", "What should I work on next?", a campaign plan. |
| **Editor** | The written word: structure, clarity, voice, headlines. | An outline, a draft, a tighter version, a check against the brand voice. |
| **Designer** | How things look: layout, color, type, images. | Feedback on a page layout, a description of the visual a post needs. |
| **Researcher** | Facts and sources. | A competitor scan, a fact check, a short brief with sources. |

The designer works in words. No design tool is connected, so it gives feedback and describes the
visual a piece needs; it does not produce the image or the layout file.

## Who decides

| Decision | Who |
| --- | --- |
| What to make | The writer, or whoever sets the team's priorities. |
| What a draft says | The writer. Claude proposes; you accept or change. |
| Whether a piece is approved | The approver. Never Claude. |
| The brand voice | The team, through a review. See [Set your brand voice](/marketing/brand-voice/). |
| When and where it is published | Your team, outside cckit. |
