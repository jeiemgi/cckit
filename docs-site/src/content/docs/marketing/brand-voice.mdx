title: Set your brand voice
description: Write your brand voice, key messages and banned phrasings down once in the project, so every draft Claude writes follows them.
---

If the voice is not written down, every draft has to be corrected by whoever remembers it, and it
has to be explained to Claude again in every conversation. Writing it down once lets every draft
start in the right voice.

**Who does it:** the team decides the voice. Claude asks the questions and writes the answers
down, and the editor assistant checks drafts against them.

**What you will have at the end:** one approved file in the project, `brand/voice.md`, that
Claude reads before it writes a draft or plans a campaign.

This is the written voice only. Logo, color and visual identity are not part of it.

## What gets recorded

| Part | What it answers | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Voice** | How do we sound? | "Direct and warm. Short sentences. We say 'you', never 'users'." |
| **Key messages** | What do we always want the reader to take away? | "Set up in a day. No contract." |
| **Banned phrasings** | What do we never say? | "Revolutionary", "best-in-class", exclamation marks in headlines. |

## Set it

<Steps>

1. **Start the conversation.**

   <div class="cckit-prompt">

   ```text title="Say this to Claude"
   Help me set our brand voice for this project. Ask me what you need to know, one question at a time.
   ```

   </div>

2. **Answer with examples.** Paste two or three pieces you think sound right, and one that sounds
   wrong. Examples tell Claude more than adjectives do. Claude first reads what the project
   already has, such as recent posts, and asks only for what that material does not answer.

3. **Read what Claude wrote back.** It shows you the voice, the key messages and the banned
   phrasings before it saves them. Correct anything that is off. A key message needs its proof: a
   number, a customer, a fact. One without proof is recorded as unproven, and Claude does not
   state it as fact in a draft.

4. **Send it for review.** The brand voice is content like any other: it goes through a draft and
   a review, so the team agrees on it before it applies to everyone.

   <div class="cckit-prompt">

   ```text title="Say this to Claude"
   Save the brand voice and ask for a review.
   ```

   </div>

</Steps>

Once it is approved, every draft and every campaign plan in this project starts from it.

## Check that it worked

Open a new conversation and ask for a draft without describing the voice. If the draft sounds
right and avoids the banned phrasings, the voice is in place.

To check one draft against it:

<div class="cckit-prompt">

```text title="Say this to Claude"
Check this draft against our brand voice. List every sentence that breaks it and propose a fix for each.
```

</div>

## Change it later

The voice changes the same way it was set: say what should change, read the result, ask for a
review. The history of every change stays in the project, with who approved it.

<Aside type="tip">
Keep the banned list short and specific. "No jargon" is hard to check. "Never say 'leverage'" is easy.
</Aside>
